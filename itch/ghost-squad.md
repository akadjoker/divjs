# Ghost Squad

**Tagline:** Pac-Man, the other way round: you are the four ghosts.

**Viewport:** 640 x 480 · **Genre:** Action · **Tags:** pac-man, arcade, maze, retro, strategy, procedural, 2d, reverse, ai-generated, pixel-art

## Description

This time the computer plays Pac-Man, and it plays well: it eats dots,
keeps to the corridors it can reach before you, and goes for the power
pills as soon as you close in - then it's your turn to run.

You steer one ghost at a time and switch whenever you like; the other
three follow their own personalities - Blinky chases, Pinky ambushes
ahead of him, Inky guards the pills and Clyde wanders. Catch him three
times before he clears the maze. Every round is a new, generated maze.

## Controls

- Arrows move your ghost
- 1-4 pick a ghost · Tab or Space next ghost
- N new maze · Space/Enter on screens

## How it was made

Written in the DIV language and running on [DivJS](https://github.com/akadjoker/divjs).
The game, the maze generator and Pac-Man's AI were written by an AI agent from the engine's reference.
