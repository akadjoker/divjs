# Dune Strike

**Tagline:** Fly an attack helicopter through a new desert every campaign: rescue pilots, hunt convoys, mind the fuel.

**Viewport:** 640 x 480 · **Genre:** Shooter · **Tags:** helicopter, shoot-em-up, desert-strike, retro, procedural, 2d, action, arcade, ai-generated, top-down

## Description

A campaign in the spirit of Desert Strike. Every campaign generates a new
desert - coast, oases, roads, bases - and five missions: take out the
radar, rescue downed pilots, clear the SAM sites, stop a supply convoy and
destroy the command bunker.

The helicopter has weight and inertia. Chain guns, Hydra rockets and
homing Hellfires each have their own ammo, and fuel runs down all the
time: hover low over fuel, ammo and armour crates to winch them aboard,
and land rescued pilots at the helipad.

## Controls

- Left/Right turn · Up thrust · Down brake
- A/D (or Shift + Left/Right) strafe
- Space guns · Z Hydra rockets · X Hellfire (homing)
- M map and briefing · Space/Enter on screens

## How it was made

Written in the DIV language and running on [DivJS](https://github.com/akadjoker/divjs).
The game and its desert generator were written by an AI agent
from the engine's reference and then played and fixed in a browser.
