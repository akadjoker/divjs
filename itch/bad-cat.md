# Bad Cat

**Tagline:** You're a cat. Everything on the shelves belongs on the floor. Don't get caught.

**Viewport:** 640 x 480 · **Genre:** Platformer · **Tags:** cat, comedy, physics, platformer, funny, cozy, 2d, procedural, ai-generated, casual

## Description

Jump from the sofa to the bookcase to the shelves and paw cups, plates,
vases, plants and the goldfish bowl over the edge. Everything is a real
physics object: it tumbles, bounces and - if it lands hard enough -
shatters, and the bigger the mess, the higher the score.

But the owner comes back now and then. You'll hear footsteps and see a
shadow under the door: hide under the table, slip behind the sofa or sit
down and look innocent. Get caught next to the wreckage three times and
you spend the night on the balcony.

Every room is furnished differently, and each one is fuller and the
owner more suspicious than the last.

## Controls

- Left/Right or A/D run · Up/W/Z/Space jump (hold for higher)
- X/J swipe · C/K sit innocently
- Down/S hide under the table or behind the sofa, or drop through furniture
- Enter start

## How it was made

Written in the DIV language and running on [DivJS](https://github.com/akadjoker/divjs),
with its physics and synthesised sound. The game, meows and music
included, was written by an AI agent from the engine's reference.
