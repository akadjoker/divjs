# Chicken Cannon

**Tagline:** A farm cannon, a fort full of grumpy foxes, and a lot of very surprised chickens.

**Viewport:** 800 x 480 · **Genre:** Puzzle · **Tags:** physics, comedy, artillery, angry-birds, chickens, casual, funny, 2d, procedural, ai-generated

## Description

Aim the cannon, set the power and launch a flapping, squawking chicken at
forts of wooden planks and straw bales, guarded by scarecrows and foxes.
Wood cracks and breaks on hard hits, straw gets shoved around, and every
level builds a new fort.

Three chickens: the bouncy hen, the heavy fat hen that smashes through
planks, and the egg bomber - click while it flies and it drops an
exploding egg. Knock out every target to clear the level, with a bonus
for every chicken you didn't need.

## Controls

- Hold the mouse button and point: the line from the cannon sets angle and power; release to fire
- Or Up/Down angle, Left/Right power, Space fire
- 1-3, Tab or click the tray to pick a chicken · click or Space in flight drops the egg
- R retry · Enter next level

## How it was made

Written in the DIV language and running on [DivJS](https://github.com/akadjoker/divjs),
with Box2D physics. The game was written by an AI agent from the
engine's reference and tested in a browser until a bot could clear the
first four levels.
