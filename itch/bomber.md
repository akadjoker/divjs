# Bomber Arena

**Tagline:** Blow up your rivals in generated arenas - chain reactions, remote bombs, cursed skulls and CPU bombers that plan their escapes.

**Viewport:** 640 x 480 · **Genre:** Action · **Tags:** bomberman, arcade, local-multiplayer, party, retro, 2d, procedural, pixel-art, ai-generated, top-down

## Description

A Bomberman-style battle game. Every round is a new arena, mirrored so
every corner is fair, in one of four themes: classic, pits you can't walk
over, conveyor belts that drag you along, and warp pads.

Drop bombs, set off chain reactions, kick bombs across the floor, detonate
them by remote - and watch out for the skull, which curses you with
reversed controls or bombs that drop by themselves until you pass it on.
When the clock runs out, sudden death drops walls in a spiral.

Play alone or with a friend against up to three CPU bombers. They share a
danger map of where every flame will be and when, and only drop a bomb
when they can see a way out.

## Controls

- **Player 1:** arrows to move · Space bomb · Enter remote detonate
- **Player 2:** WASD · Q bomb · E remote
- Menu: Up/Down choose, Left/Right change, Space/Enter start · Esc title

## How it was made

Written in the DIV language and running on [DivJS](https://github.com/akadjoker/divjs).
The whole game, CPU players included, was written by an AI agent
from the engine's reference and then tested in a browser.
