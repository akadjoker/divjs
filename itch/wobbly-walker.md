# Wobbly Walker

**Tagline:** Four keys, two legs, one hundred metres. Good luck.

**Viewport:** 640 x 480 · **Genre:** Sports · **Tags:** qwop, ragdoll, physics, comedy, sports, running, difficult, funny, 2d, ai-generated

## Description

A QWOP-style running game. Your runner is a ragdoll of physics bodies
held together by joints with motors: Q and W swing the thighs, O and P
bend the knees, and nothing else keeps them upright.

Every attempt gets a new bumpy track with hurdles that tip over when you
kick them. Land on your face and the crowd goes "oooh". Reach 100 metres
and you're a legend.

## Controls

- Q / W swing the thighs · O / P bend the knees
- R restart · Space try again after a fall

## How it was made

Written in the DIV language and running on [DivJS](https://github.com/akadjoker/divjs),
with Box2D joints and motors. The game was written by an AI agent
from the engine's reference. It's deliberately hard: our test bot never
got past three metres.
